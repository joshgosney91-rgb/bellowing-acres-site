Our Mission

Every Animal Deserves a Chance

Bellowing Acres provides a forever home to rescued nuisance alligators destined for euthanasia, rehabilitated animals that legally cannot be re-released, and surrendered exotic pets. Through education and awareness, we teach people how to co-exist with wildlife and why apex predators matter.

🏡
Forever Homes Every animal that arrives at Bellowing Acres stays for life — no transfers, no exceptions.
🎓
Education First We believe co-existence starts with understanding. We host tours and outreach to change minds.
🤝
Community Partnerships We work with trappers, wildlife agencies, and rescues to give animals a path forward.

Why It Matters

Why Do Alligators
Need Rescuing?

Florida is home to over a million wild alligators — and every year, tens of thousands of nuisance calls flood wildlife agencies. When an alligator is deemed a "nuisance," they face a grim reality: state law prohibits relocation of animals over four feet, leaving only two options. Bellowing Acres chooses the third.

📞

Thousands of nuisance calls per year

Wildlife agencies receive a staggering number of calls annually from Floridians reporting alligators near homes, roads, and waterways.

📏

Animals over 4ft cannot be relocated

Florida law prohibits releasing nuisance gators back into the wild. Once removed, they can never return to a natural habitat.

⚖️

Captivity or euthanasia — the only options

Without sanctuaries like Bellowing Acres, these animals are killed. We exist to offer a life worth living instead.

"These animals aren't dangerous because they're evil — they're doing exactly what alligators have done for 37 million years. They're just trying to survive. We believe they deserve the chance to keep doing that."

— Chris Gillette, Co-Founder
