Our Story

What began as a passion for wildlife became a 40-acre forever home for Florida's most misunderstood animals.

How It Started

Born from a Love of
Florida's Wild Places

Bellowing Acres didn't start with a business plan. It started with a phone call.

Chris Gillette had spent his entire life immersed in Florida's wild spaces — a childhood spent catching snakes in the Everglades, a career built on getting closer to the animals most people run from. Gabby Scampone had spent 15 years rescuing animals, fighting for second chances, and learning everything there was to know about caring for wildlife in need.

When nuisance gator after nuisance gator began coming their way — animals with nowhere to go but a bullet — they made a decision. Not just to rescue one, or five, or ten. But to build something permanent. Something that would outlast them.

In 2021, Bellowing Acres Wildlife Sanctuary was officially established in Putnam County, Florida. In 2023, they purchased the 40 acres that would become the permanent home of more than 100 animals. Today, the sanctuary continues to grow — in land, in residents, and in mission.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2021

Bellowing Acres Founded

Chris Gillette and Gabby Scampone officially established Bellowing Acres Wildlife Sanctuary as a Florida nonprofit. The first rescue animals arrived within months — nuisance alligators that would have otherwise been euthanized.

2022

Growing the Herd

The sanctuary took in its first major wave of rescues — a mix of nuisance gators from across North Florida and surrendered exotic pets. Word spread through trapper networks and wildlife agencies. The demand for a sanctuary like this was undeniable.

2023

40 Acres Purchased

A landmark moment: Chris and Gabby purchased 40 acres of natural Florida habitat in Putnam County — giving the sanctuary the space it needed to grow. Enclosure construction began immediately, with plans to house hundreds of animals over time.

2023

Akila Joins the Family

Gatorland Orlando donated Akila, a magnificent Nile crocodile, to the sanctuary — a major milestone that elevated Bellowing Acres' profile and cemented its reputation as a serious, long-term facility for rare species.

2024

501(c)(3) Status Pursued

Bellowing Acres began the process of registering as a federal 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization — a step toward greater transparency, increased donation capacity, and long-term sustainability for the sanctuary's mission.

Present

100+ Animals. Still Growing.

Today, Bellowing Acres is home to over 100 animals — alligators, crocodiles, tortoises, birds, reptiles, and more. The mission hasn't changed: every animal that arrives here gets a forever home. That will never change.

The Team

The People Behind the Mission

🧑‍🔬

Chris Gillette

Co-Founder · Wildlife Biologist · Animal Handler

Chris Gillette is one of the most respected names in American reptile handling. Born and raised in Florida, he has spent his entire life in pursuit of the animals most people fear — and working to change that fear into understanding.

A wildlife biologist by training and a photographer by passion, Chris has worked hands-on with crocodiles, alligators, sharks, venomous snakes, and dozens of other apex predators. He is known worldwide for his extraordinary ability to train and habituate large crocodilians — a rare and delicate skill that forms the operational backbone of Bellowing Acres.

Chris has appeared in national media, collaborated with wildlife agencies across Florida, and has built a massive following through his social media presence — using every platform to advocate for the animals he loves and the sanctuary he built.

👩‍🦰

Gabby Scampone

Co-Founder · Animal Rescuer · Sanctuary Operations

Gabby Scampone has spent over 15 years doing what most people would find overwhelming: giving second chances to animals in crisis. Her background spans domestic animal rescue, exotic wildlife care, and wildlife rehabilitation — a diverse expertise that makes her an irreplaceable force at Bellowing Acres.

While Chris brings the biology and handling expertise, Gabby brings the heart of the operation. She manages the daily care routines, coordinates with veterinarians and rescuers, and ensures that every animal at the sanctuary receives the attention, enrichment, and love it deserves.

Gabby's belief — that every living creature deserves a chance — is not a bumper sticker sentiment. It's a daily practice that visitors see and feel the moment they walk through the gate.

Partnerships

Our Partners & Network

We don't do this alone. Our network of partners, trappers, and fellow rescuers makes every save possible.

🐍

Swamp Girl Reptile Rescue

A trusted partner in reptile rescue and rehabilitation. We coordinate regularly to ensure surrendered animals find the right permanent placement.

🐊

Gatorland Orlando

Donated Akila, our beloved Nile crocodile. Gatorland's trust in Bellowing Acres speaks to the quality of care we provide for rare and large species.

🧢

Licensed Nuisance Trappers

Florida's licensed nuisance alligator trappers are our most important pipeline. Instead of euthanizing, they call us. We pick up. That's how lives are saved.

🏛️

Florida Fish & Wildlife (FWC)

We work within FWC's regulatory framework to ensure all animals in our care are properly permitted and tracked. Compliance is non-negotiable.

🩺

Veterinary Partners

Our relationships with exotic animal veterinarians across North Florida ensure every animal receives prompt, expert medical care when needed.

🌎

Wildlife Educators

We collaborate with educators, schools, and conservation organizations to spread our message about coexistence and the importance of apex predators.

What We Believe

Our Values & Philosophy

These aren't rules we follow. They're the beliefs that drive every decision we make.

🏡

Forever, Not Temporary

No animal at Bellowing Acres is ever rehomed, transferred, or removed unless medically necessary. When we say "forever home," we mean it — for the life of the animal.

🔬

Science-Based Care

Every enclosure, diet, and husbandry decision is grounded in the best available science and years of hands-on experience. Our animals receive expert care, not guesswork.

🧠

Education Changes Everything

Fear drives most human-wildlife conflict. Understanding resolves it. We believe that every person who walks through our gates leaves as a wildlife advocate.

🌿

Respect for Wild Nature

Our animals are not performers. They are wild animals living in managed care. We honor their nature, their instincts, and their individuality — always.

🤝

Radical Transparency

We are 100% open about how donations are used, how animals are acquired, and how the sanctuary operates. You deserve to know exactly where your support goes.

💛

Every Life Has Value

An 11-foot nuisance gator. A surrendered pet tortoise. A confiscated baby crocodile. Every one of them matters. That belief is non-negotiable and never changes.
