Your Safety

Safety is Our Top Priority

🛡️

Expert-Guided Tours Only

All tours are conducted by experienced, licensed animal handlers. Guests never interact with animals unsupervised. Our team has decades of combined experience working with crocodilians and exotic wildlife.

🦺

Proper Footwear

Closed-toe shoes required. We recommend comfortable walking shoes or boots. The terrain can be uneven.

🚶

Guests must remain with the guide at all times. No wandering off designated paths during the tour.

🧒

Family Friendly

FAQ

Common Questions

What should I wear?

Comfortable, closed-toe shoes are required — the property covers 40 acres of natural Florida terrain. Light, breathable clothing is recommended, especially in summer. Bug spray is a great idea.

Can I bring kids?

Absolutely! We love hosting families. Children must be accompanied by an adult at all times. This is an incredible educational experience for kids of all ages.

Is it safe?

Yes. All tours are guided by experienced, licensed animal handlers. Guests observe animals from safe distances or designated interaction zones. Safety is non-negotiable for us.

How long is the tour?

Tours typically run 90 minutes to 2 hours, depending on group size and the number of questions. We never rush — every animal's story deserves to be told.

Can I take photos?

Yes! Photography is encouraged. We ask that you refrain from using flash near the animals. Many guests bring professional cameras — the animals make for incredible subjects.

Can I feed the animals?

Feeding is done only by trained staff. However, some tour experiences include a supervised feeding session — ask about this when booking!

Do you host school groups?

Yes! We offer educational programs tailored for school groups of all ages. Contact us to discuss a curriculum-aligned visit for your class or organization.

What's the booking process?

Submit the form above or email us. We'll confirm availability, share pricing, and send directions. Full payment is due before or on the day of the tour.
